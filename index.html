Reading the projections

  • Confirmed items are announced for 2026 on the temple site. Projected items are placed from 2024-2025 recurrence and will shift a few days once each month's 行事予定 post appears (usually on the 1st).
  • The 秋月祭 Autumn Moon Festival (Sep 25-26) is tied to the mid-autumn moon and runs on special evening admission. It is the season's single highest-impact date.
  • The autumn light-up あたら夜もみじ and peak 紅葉 (~Nov 13-30) are the busiest stretch of the six months. 2025 ran Nov 14-30; re-check the exact 2026 dates in late October.
  • 紫式部法要 tracks the mid-autumn moon, so in 2026 it likely coincides with the festival on Sep 25 rather than falling in early October as it did in 2025.
  • 除夜の鐘 (Dec 31) and 初詣 (Jan 1-3) are standard year-end / New-Year crowds; exact timings not yet posted.
